# Overview
Short applet made for scraping culturele-vacatures.nl.
- Scrapes job listings from websites
- Filters based on keywords, earlier results and application deadlines to provide a list of new job listings
- Provides an overview of keypoints per job listing to approve or delete the entry
- Summarizes results to send per mail
